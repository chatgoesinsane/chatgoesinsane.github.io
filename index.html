Clip-Zentrale

Clip-Zentrale is a private desktop tool used only by its owner to manage the short-form video channel Chat Goes Insane (@chatgoesinsane) on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

What it does:

  • Uploads short video clips created by the owner to the owner's own YouTube channel, TikTok account (as drafts) and Instagram account.
  • Reads the view counts of the owner's own videos to track performance.

It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not access data of other users.
