Clip-Zentrale

Clip-Zentrale is a private desktop tool used only by its owner to manage the short-form video channel Chat Goes Insane (@chatgoesinsane).

What it does:

  • Uploads short video clips created by the owner to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts, which are reviewed and posted manually in the TikTok app.
  • Reads the view counts of the owner's own videos to track performance.

It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not access data of other TikTok users.
