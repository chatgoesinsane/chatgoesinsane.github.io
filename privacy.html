Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Overview

Clip-Zentrale is a private desktop tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own channel Chat Goes Insane (@chatgoesinsane) on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. It has no other users.

2. YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

After the owner signs in with Google (OAuth 2.0), the app uses these YouTube API Services:

  • Upload videos (scope youtube.upload, endpoint videos.insert): uploads the owner's own clips to the owner's own channel.
  • Read channel and video statistics (scope youtube.readonly, endpoints channels.list and videos.list): reads the channel name and the view counts of the owner's own videos.

The app does not access, collect or process YouTube data of any other users or channels.

3. TikTok and Instagram

For TikTok the app uses basic profile information (user.info.basic), uploads the owner's own videos as drafts (video.upload) and reads the list and view counts of the owner's own videos (video.list). For Instagram it uses the official Instagram Graph API to publish the owner's own Reels and read their view counts.

4. What is stored, and where

Access tokens, refresh tokens, video IDs, links and view counts are stored only locally on the owner's computer. They are not sent to any server operated by the app and are not shared with or sold to third parties. The app does not use cookies or tracking.

Data is exchanged only with the official APIs of YouTube (Google), TikTok and Instagram (Meta).

5. Retention and deletion

While the YouTube account is connected, the stored YouTube view counts are refreshed automatically every day. If the account is disconnected, its locally stored tokens are deleted immediately by the app, and the owner deletes the remaining stored YouTube API data (video IDs and view counts) within 30 days.

6. Revoking access

  • Google / YouTube: at any time via the Google security settings page (https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Instagram: in Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites.
