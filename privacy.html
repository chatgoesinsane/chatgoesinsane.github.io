Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Overview

Clip-Zentrale is a private desktop tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own channel Chat Goes Insane (@chatgoesinsane). It has no other users.

2. Data the app accesses

After the owner signs in with TikTok, the app accesses only the owner's own account data that is needed for its function:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic), to show which account is connected.
  • Uploading the owner's own videos as drafts (video.upload).
  • The list and view counts of the owner's own videos (video.list), to track performance.

The app does not access, collect or process data of any other TikTok users.

3. Storage

Access tokens and statistics are stored only locally on the owner's computer. They are not sent to any server operated by the app and are not shared with or sold to third parties.

4. Third parties

Data is exchanged only with the official APIs of TikTok and the other platforms the owner uses. Their own privacy policies apply.

5. Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app settings (Security → Manage app permissions). Locally stored data is deleted by disconnecting the account in the app.
